01 / Overview

THE PROJECT

My first album cover project for a client—creating a brand-new visual identity for Reydor's release.

This was my first album cover design created for a client. Rather than a redesign or speculative concept, it was an original cover designed specifically for the artist's upcoming release.

The objective was to establish a distinctive visual identity that captured the soul of the track, translating the song's underlying emotional intensity into artwork that stood out across streaming platforms and physical music formats.

02 / Concept

RAGE OF LOVE

Interpreting the song's emotional atmosphere as a raw collision of love, intensity and rage.

When listening to the track, the emotional atmosphere felt deeply volatile—a complex interplay where tenderness collides with uninhibited passion. I interpreted this core feeling as the rage of love.

The creative direction was built directly around that interpretation. Stark pitch blacks, saturated crimson reds, and high-contrast photographic portraiture were composed together to evoke both classical romance and sudden emotional ferocity.

Primary digital single artwork (left) and physical cassette packaging translation (right).

03 / Palette

THE COLOUR STORY

A bold contrast of vintage red, monochrome imagery and electric yellow creates the visual identity of the release.

Sampled directly from the master Kabhi Kabhi album artwork, these five tones establish the emotional friction of the release. Saturated vintage red and pitch black anchor the visual weight, while warm grey and charcoal soften the analog portrait texture, punctuated by razor-sharp electric yellow typography.

04 / Craft & Execution

FROM IDEA TO ARTWORK

Crafted in Canva, focusing on bold typographic weight, analog texture and emotive imagery.

Since this was my first client project, Canva was the design software I utilized at the time to bring the concept to life. Working within Canva, I developed the entire visual composition independently while incorporating the client's direct feedback and project requirements.

While earlier working iterations were lost over time, the delivered artwork contains deliberate micro-details that embody the project's artistic vision:

Artwork Detail 01

Classical Portraiture & Romance

A monochrome vintage portrait of a woman with a wide-brimmed hat holding a floral bouquet, anchoring the classical romantic core.

Artwork Detail 02

Typographic Tension & Palette

High-contrast condensed "KABHI KABHI" letterforms framed by vivid crimson stripes, accented by the subtle yellow "reydor" signature.

Packaging Adaptation

Analog Physical Archive

Translating the 2D digital album cover into tactile transparent cassette media with custom labels and spine typography.

Sole responsibility for creative direction, artwork execution and client delivery.

From the initial brief to the final master files, I directed and created the overall artwork for Reydor's release:

Interpreted the Song's Direction

Analyzed the track's musical pacing and lyrical tone to define its core thematic focus: the rage of love.

02

03

Designed the Album Cover

Created the entire visual artwork independently using Canva, balancing artistic expression with release requirements.

04

Incorporated Client Feedback

Collaborated closely with the artist, refining details and layout elements based on Reydor's input.

05

07 / Reflection

A FIRST

A foundational step in my growth as an independent visual designer.

"This project was my first client album cover. It was an invaluable opportunity to translate an intangible emotional feeling into a cohesive visual identity, while learning to balance my own creative intuition with client feedback and real-world release requirements."

Working within the tools I had at the time, the project reinforced that strong creative direction stems from clear conceptual interpretation, careful composition, and listening deeply to the emotional core of the work.
