SATYAJIT RAY — T-SHIRT DESIGN

APPAREL & MERCHANDISE DESIGN

This is a T-shirt design inspired by Satyajit Ray, featuring Bengali typography and a hand-drawn character illustration.

+ + + +
PROJECT TYPE Freelance Project
CATEGORY Apparel & Merchandise Design
INSPIRATION Satyajit Ray
MEDIUM Screenprint on Apparel
01 // VISUAL BREAKDOWN

ELEMENTS & INSPIRATION

Honoring the visual and literary world of Satyajit Ray, this freelance apparel design balances expressive hand-drawn illustration with literary Bengali letterforms across a neutral textile canvas.

01 // CHEST LETTERFORMS

Bengali Typography

"ভয় না পেলে তারি আগিবান্রের বড়ো শৌন হর পীরভম না !"

Printed across the upper chest, the Bengali quote carries the distinctive character of Ray's literary voice, scaled with deliberate editorial restraint against the garment's neckline.

02 // LOWER HEM ARTWORK

Hand-Drawn Character

Anchored at the lower front hem, the character illustration pays homage to Ray's iconic illustrated protagonists and folkloric visual universe—complete with expressive eyes, spiked crown details, scroll banner lettering, and curled shoes.
